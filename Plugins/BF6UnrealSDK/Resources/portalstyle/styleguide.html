The chamfered ring

The signature hover and focus affordance: a 2px ring drawn on a ::before inset outside the control, with the four corners cut off by a clip-path polygon. It is not a radius; it is a chamfer. Inset: -5px on buttons, -4px on inputs and icon buttons. Read from .button-module_button__51-il:hover:before.
