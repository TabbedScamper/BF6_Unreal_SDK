What the readouts mean
PAGE NOT OPEN means the Portal blocks page is not open in the panel, so nothing can be sent to it. NOT SIGNED IN means Portal has not been signed in to. Neither is about your internet connection, and your work is safe here either way: it goes over as soon as the page is open.
A style readout that says the offline default means the tool is drawing with what it recovered from the public site rather than from your own signed-in page. Press MATCH THE SITE'S LOOK under MORE to make it exact.
